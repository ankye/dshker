## What the Windows verification covered

On Windows 11 (10.0.26200), with a rebuilt managed worktree (`66491b05e3`,
Harness 0.2.2-rc.1) and an isolated `DSH_HOME`:

- `collectRoots('win32')` → C/D/E/F; the home drive is excluded from the rows;
- the real `dsh web` loaded the Launcher overlay and the probe overlay; home
  listed the D, E and F drive roots first and each listed real children (39/25/26);
- the real "选择工作区目录" dialog showed the three rows over the home folders,
  and selecting the D drive switched the dialog to that disk with its real contents
  (screenshots `dsh-win-ui-4.png`, `dsh-win-ui-5.png`).

The same machine's managed checkout had 172 unreadable workspace junctions and
could not start DSH at all; it was rebuilt (`git worktree add` +
`pnpm install --frozen-lockfile --registry https://registry.npmmirror.com` +
`pnpm run build`) and the leftover tree purged link-by-link. That incident is
what motivated the readiness proof shipped here.
